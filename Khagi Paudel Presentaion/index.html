About Nepali Culture

Nepal is a multicultural country with many languages, traditions, festivals and ethnic communities. Nepali culture is famous for its diversity and hospitality.

🎉

Festivals

Dashain, Tihar, Holi and many other festivals are celebrated throughout Nepal.

🍛

Nepali Food

Dal Bhat, Momo, Sel Roti and Gundruk are popular Nepali foods.

👘

Traditional Dress

Daura Suruwal, Gunyu Cholo and traditional jewelry are important parts of Nepali culture.

🙏

Hospitality

Nepali people are known for welcoming guests with warmth and respect.
